💡 Getting Accurate Prices in Seconds

Step 1: 📁 Drop Your Sliced File (.gcode or .3mf)

Export your sliced plate directly from Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer, or Cura. Dropping the file auto-fills your printer profile, runtime, filament weights, and individual AMS spool colors.

Step 2: ⚠️ Why Purge & Waste Matters

On multi-color prints, flushed ‘poop’ and wipe towers can easily equal 40%–60% of your total filament. Most makers lose money by only charging for the finished model weight. We automatically detect and price your purged plastic so the client pays for the true material cost.

Step 3: ⚡ Machine Wear & Power

We factor in your printer’s average sustained wattage (not peak supply) and hourly hardware wear/depreciation so your equipment pays for its own maintenance and nozzles over time.

Step 4: 📈 Markup Guidelines

  • Wholesale (40%): Best for print-farm bulk batches (20+ units).
  • Retail / DTC (100%): Standard for Etsy, craft fairs, and single items (50% gross margin).
  • Prototyping / Custom (200%): Best for one-off CAD jobs, rush orders, or complex engineering prints.
